Our Story

Smriti Rentals was founded in 2010 with a simple goal: make car rental stress-free, transparent, and genuinely enjoyable. What started as a fleet of 12 vehicles in Auckland has grown into a nationwide operation with over 200 vehicles and 6 branches.

We're proud to be one of the few car rental companies in New Zealand actively investing in an electric and hybrid fleet - because we believe the future of travel is clean, quiet, and sustainable.

"We don't just rent cars - we deliver the freedom to explore on your terms."
- Smriti Bhandari, Founder & CEO
